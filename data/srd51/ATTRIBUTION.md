- Edition: D&D 5e **2014** (SRD 5.1). SRD 5.2.x is a different document and is **not** used.
- `srd51.json` is generated by `node scripts/srd/import-srd.mjs <pdf>`; it is a structured extraction (spells, monsters, weapons, armor, adventuring gear, class tables and class text, races, one background, one feat, subclass names). Changes: text re-flowed, hyphenation normalised, structured into fields.
- Not included, by design: anything outside the SRD (Player's Handbook-only backgrounds such as Criminal, other subclasses, Xanathar's, Tasha's, …) and any Russian translation. Russian names shown in the app for items and creature keywords are community terminology for display only, are not part of the SRD and are unverified.
- Known extraction gaps are listed in `meta.warnings` inside the file (Giant Fly stat block, Ranger level table) and in `docs/limitations.md`.
